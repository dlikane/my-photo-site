Dmitry Likane is a portrait, fashion, and music photographer based in Melbourne, Australia.

“I love capturing people. Whether it’s an everyday individual, a high-fashion model, or someone lost in the rhythm of music, I aim to reveal the spark that makes them unique.”

His work blends art and personality, spanning from moody portraits to dynamic performance shots. With a background in philosophy and a lifelong fascination with light, his images invite you to look a little closer — to find the story behind the glance, the energy behind the stillness.

The Focus

Dmitry doesn’t just chase beauty; he seeks truth in expression—the quiet, the bold, the raw, and the rare. His sessions often feel like conversations, drawing out authenticity through a mix of art direction, timing, and intuition.

If you're after photography that blends style, soul, and a touch of cinematic drama, you're in the right place.
