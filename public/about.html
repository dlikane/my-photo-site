Dmitry Likane is a Melbourne-based photographer exploring portraiture, fashion, and music with a cinematic touch and a love for honest expression.

“I photograph people not just to show how they look — but to reveal something deeper. Whether it’s a quiet moment, a bold pose, or a flash of mischief, I want to catch the spark that feels real.”

Blending artistry and instinct, Dmitry’s work moves between carefully lit studio setups and spontaneous on-location shots. From timeless black-and-white portraits to bold, stylized editorials, he creates images that are both striking and personal.

The Focus

Dmitry’s sessions are relaxed but intentional — part collaboration, part creative play. He uses lighting, styling, and mood to build visual stories where the model feels present and powerful.

With an eye for detail, texture, and mood, his photos are shaped as much by conversation as by composition. If you're looking for images that combine depth, drama, and a sense of connection — welcome.
